# Qwen3-ASR as a local STT engine — what was measured, 2026-09-09

Short version: **it works, it is faster than our whisper on the same
machine, it is more accurate on hard conversational Italian, and it needs
no new dependency.** What it does not yet have is any wiring into the
product.

## The thing that was wrong for a day

Qwen3-ASR was written off twice on this branch before the facts arrived:

1. *"our llama.cpp does not have the ASR projector"* — wrong. It landed
   upstream on **2026-04-12** (PR #19441, *"mtmd: qwen3 audio support
   (qwen3-omni and qwen3-asr)"*) and our vendored copy is from
   **2026-08-19**. The search missed it because the code is named
   `qwen3a`: ONE projector serves both Qwen3-Omni and Qwen3-ASR.
2. *"we need to bump llama-cpp-4"* — also wrong, twice over. The fork we
   depend on carries the SAME llama.cpp submodule (`0adcc3bb`) at its
   HEAD as at our pin, so a bump moves nothing; and nothing needed moving.

Three checks settle it, and they are cheap to repeat:

| what the model asks for | what the vendored tree has |
|---|---|
| `clip.audio.projector_type = qwen3a` | `clip-impl.h`: `{ PROJECTOR_TYPE_QWEN3A, "qwen3a" }` |
| `general.architecture = qwen3vl` | `tools/mtmd/models/qwen3vl.cpp` |
| the Qwen3-ASR audio encoder | `tools/mtmd/models/qwen3a.cpp`, which cites `QwenLM/Qwen3-ASR` |

## What actually had to change

`mtmd` is an OPTIONAL cargo feature of `llama-cpp-4`. It is on by default
upstream, and we build the crate with `default-features = false`, so it
was compiled OUT — the shipped install has `llama.dll` and `ggml*.dll` and
no `mtmd.dll`. The whole dependency-side change is the new
`local-stt-qwen` feature that turns it on.

Cost: **4 minutes of build and one 2.5 MB DLL.** `mtmd.dll` depends only
on `llama.dll` + `ggml*.dll`, which we already ship. The other libraries
that appear in `target/release` (`llama-bench-impl`, `DirectML`, …) are
build byproducts and are not linked by `dimmy_lib.dll`.

## Two things that cost an hour each

* **The chat template is not optional.** Fed a raw prompt the model emits
  end-of-generation on the FIRST token and the transcript comes back
  empty — which is indistinguishable from silence, so it looks like a
  broken audio path. `apply_chat_template` (the same call `local_llm`
  makes) fixes it.
* **`local-stt-qwen` must imply `dynamic-link`.** Statically linked,
  `mtmd-helper.cpp` references `hash_sha256_hex` and llama-cpp-sys-4 does
  not ship its definition in the rlib: `LNK2019`. Clippy never links, so
  it passes; `cargo test --lib` is where it dies.

## Measurements — this machine, this audio

Hardware: NVIDIA T600 Laptop (4 GB), Vulkan. Audio: the mic track of a
real Italian meeting, `43bc6a8c`, with our own whisper transcript of the
same audio as the reference.

**Speed, warm, model resident, through OUR build** (`qwen_asr_smoke`):

| window | time | realtime factor |
|---|---|---|
| 15 s (first, shader warmup) | 3.71 s | 4.0x |
| 15 s | 2.59 s | 5.8x |
| 15 s | 2.36 s | 6.3x |
| 15 s | 2.32 s | 6.5x |
| 3 s (dictation) | 0.78 s | 3.8x |
| 5 s (dictation) | 1.15 s | 4.4x |

Model load is 4.7 s, paid once.

**Against whisper, same 15 s chunk, same session** (`bench_local`):

| engine | time |
|---|---|
| whisper Tiny | 6847 ms |
| whisper Base | 1483 ms |
| whisper Small | 1701 ms |
| whisper Large-v3-Turbo Q8 | 5870 ms |
| **Qwen3-ASR 1.7B** | **2590 ms** |

Whisper ran there in auto-detect and answered in English, which is its
known local-autodetect defect and not the product path — the product
forces the language. The timings stand regardless.

**Accuracy**, against the whisper transcript of the same meeting:

| said | whisper turbo | Qwen3-ASR 1.7B |
|---|---|---|
| "tag NFC" | "tag NFC", once "del FC" | "tag NFC" |
| "non e` IMpossibile ma piu` difficile" | "non e` possibile ma e` difficile" | "non e` impossibile ma piu` difficile" |
| "non riesci ad avere qualcosa di piu` strutturato" | "non riesce… non riuscire" | correct |

The middle row is the reason to care: whisper wrote the OPPOSITE of what
was said, and that sentence goes into a recap unchallenged.

**Two defects of its own:**

* The answer is prefixed `language Italian<asr_text>…` (llama.cpp
  #26749). `qwen_asr::strip_asr_scaffolding` is the single place that
  undoes it, and the language verdict is worth keeping — it is the same
  answer `lang_detect` runs a separate whisper pass to get.
* Near-silence hallucinates, in Chinese. On the window where whisper
  produced *"Eftir það er hann"* and *"Grazie a tutti"*, Qwen produced
  `哎，我操！哦，嗯。`. It is a wash, and the existing chunk VAD gate must
  stay in front of it unchanged.

**Also structural, and in our favour:** cost tracks the audio you give
it. Whisper pads every input to a 30 s encoder window, so a 3 s dictation
costs what 30 s costs — the waste measured on 2026-07-31. Qwen3-ASR does
a 3 s utterance in 0.78 s and a 15 s one in 2.4 s.

## Window size: 30 s, not 15 s

The meeting path chunks whisper at 15 s. Qwen3-ASR should not inherit that
number. Measured over the same 120 s, same model, same session:

| window | "tag NFC" | "non e` IMpossibile" | punctuation | realtime |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15 s | 1 of 3 (else "tag N S C", "NFT") | lost, becomes "non e` possibile" | poor | 5.3-6.6x |
| 30 s | 2 of 2 in the window that has them | still lost | good | 5.1-6.8x |
| 120 s (whole clip) | every one | correct | absent | 4.1x |

Two things follow. Accuracy rises with context and never falls, so the
acronyms we lose at 15 s are a windowing choice, not a model limit. And it
is FREE: the realtime factor is flat across window sizes, because the
encoder cost is proportional and the decode is what dominates. The only
price is latency -- a meeting transcript that updates every 30 s instead of
every 15. Dictation is unaffected: there the window is the utterance.

The 120 s row also explains the earlier "it has no punctuation" claim,
which was wrong: punctuation is present at 15 s and good at 30 s, and only
the single-shot 120 s run drops it.

## What exists now, and what does not

Landed on `feat/qwen3-asr-mtmd`:

* `local-stt-qwen` feature (mtmd + dynamic-link).
* `core/src/qwen_asr.rs` — `strip_asr_scaffolding` (pure, 6 unit tests)
  and `QwenAsr::{load, transcribe}`, holding model + projector so the
  residency is explicit.
* `core/src/local_llm.rs` — `shared_backend()`, because `LlamaBackend` is
  a process-wide singleton and a second `init()` fails: a local recap
  followed by a local transcription would otherwise be the failing pair.
* `core/src/bin/qwen_asr_smoke.rs` — the measurement above.

NOT done, deliberately:

* No catalog entry, no download, no routing, no UI. `local_stt_backend`
  is still whisper/parakeet.
* **The feature is NOT in any release workflow's feature set.** Turning
  it on there would ship 2.5 MB for something nothing calls yet.
* Memory: the model is 1.02 GB (0.6B) to ~2.5 GB (1.7B) and would sit
  next to a local LLM. On the 4 GB card measured here, that contention is
  exactly what made everything crawl until the GPU was reset — VRAM was
  93% occupied and nothing fit.
